### Step 2: NB1B Label-Conditioned Rationale Generation (`nb1b_teacher.ipynb` — GPU T4 × 2)
1. Open `notebooks/nb1b_teacher.ipynb`. Settings: **GPU T4 × 2**, **Internet ON**.
2. Loads `Qwen/Qwen2.5-Coder-14B-Instruct` (or `Qwen/Qwen3-14B`) in 4-bit NF4 with `enable_thinking=False`.
3. **Timed Smoke Mode:**
   - In smoke mode (`CFG.smoke_test = True`), processes 20 samples and prints per-item latency:
     `[SMOKE nb1b] Item X/20: Y.YYs (avg Z.ZZs/item) | Extrapolated total: W.W min`
4. Runs Ground-Truth Anchored distillation across training records:
   - Generates 3-step structured output (Step 1 reasoning, Step 2 JSON, Step 3 sandwich feedback).
   - Validates format and checks marks against gold labels.
   - Falls back to verified code-generated trace (`src/trace_builder.py`) if teacher output fails validation.
5. Produces: `/kaggle/working/output/teacher_distilled/train_sft_distilled.jsonl`.
